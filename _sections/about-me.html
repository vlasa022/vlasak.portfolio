Hello! I am a Master of Geographic Information Science (MGIS) student at the University of Minnesota and a Graduate Research Assistant with the Polar Geospatial Center. My academic work focuses on applying GIS, remote sensing, spatial analysis, and geospatial data management to support research, resource stewardship, and preservation. My interest in geospatial technologies began through archaeology and cultural resource preservation, where I was introduced to LiDAR, satellite imagery, and other remote sensing survey methods as tools for identifying and understanding cultural landscapes.
